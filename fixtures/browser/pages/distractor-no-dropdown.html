Licensing FAQ

Answers to common questions about how this project is licensed. There is nothing to select on this page — it is reference reading only.


What licence is the open surface under?

The engines, trace, and CLI directories are Apache-2.0. The fixture pages and browser benchmark data ship under the same permissive terms.

Can I use it commercially?

Yes. Apache-2.0 permits commercial use, modification, and distribution, provided the notice and licence text are retained.

Why is the scoring core closed?

The suites, judge, stats, and versioning engines remain proprietary while their interfaces are still moving; only the outer surface is opened.
